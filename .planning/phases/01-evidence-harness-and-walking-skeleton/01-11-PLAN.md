## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** developer, **I want to** run one command that builds the core and enforces the rights and determinism boundaries, **so that** I have proof that a skeleton machine is repeatable and refuses bad input without side effects.

<objective>
Make the check fail closed on rights-bearing content (FND-04) and on PII or credentials (the owner's condition for the public repository):
- a portable binary scan that implements the CONTEXT rule as amended on 2026-11-09 (plan-check B-1): "listed in a manifest" is satisfied only by a fixture manifest SHA-256, by the pin zone's per-file list derived from the verified archive, or, for toolchain-output magic, by location inside the CMake binary dir's own output paths; nothing else is exempt, and the exact-512-byte rule applies everywhere;
- a PII and credential scan over the tree and the history.
Each is proven by seeded controls in an isolated temporary tree. RESEARCH open question 2 (F2) is resolved by that amendment.

Purpose: no firmware-like, unmanifested or private content can reach the public repository or CI artifacts without the check failing.
Output: `scan-binaries.sh`, `hygiene.sh`, two control scripts, the `artifacts` and `history` steps in `check.sh` and four inventory rows.

**Open conflict RC-1 (blocks Task 3 only).** CONTEXT also fixes "build cc65 into `build/pins/`" (plan 01-10). That build adds roughly 1,700 files to `build/pins/cc65/` that are not on the archive's per-file list, including Mach-O/ELF host tools in `bin/`, cc65-format objects and `lib/lynx.lib` (not reproducible, RESEARCH Pitfall 2). Under the amended rule as written, all of them fail, so the `artifacts` step cannot be green while cc65 is built there. Resolving this needs an owner/orchestrator decision recorded in `01-CONTEXT.md`; this plan adds no exemption on its own. Tasks 1 and 2 do not depend on it.
</objective>
