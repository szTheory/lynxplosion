## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** developer, **I want to** run one command that builds the core and enforces the rights and determinism boundaries, **so that** I have proof that a skeleton machine is repeatable and refuses bad input without side effects.

<objective>
Make the check fail closed on rights-bearing content (FND-04) and on PII or credentials (the owner's condition for the public repository):
- a portable binary scan over source, pin and build-artifact zones with a hash-keyed manifest allowlist and a hard 512-byte rule;
- a PII and credential scan over the tree and the history.
Each is proven by seeded controls in an isolated temporary repository.

Purpose: no firmware-like, unmanifested or private content can reach the public repository or CI artifacts without the check failing.
Output: `scan-binaries.sh`, `hygiene.sh`, two control scripts, two check steps and four inventory rows.
</objective>
