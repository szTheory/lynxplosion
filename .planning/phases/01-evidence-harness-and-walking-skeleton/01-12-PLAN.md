## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** developer, **I want to** run one command that builds the core and enforces the rights and determinism boundaries, **so that** I have proof that a skeleton machine is repeatable and refuses bad input without side effects.

<objective>
Write down and machine-check the rights and evidence rules:
- the draft clean-room policy and source-exposure record (FND-07, draft pending O6) with a provenance notice for copied helpers;
- a claim template plus a checked example claim bound to the exact test inventory (FND-08).

Purpose: rights and evidence rules are written down and checked before any accuracy work starts. This plan is the last one to change `tests/inventory/fast.txt` in Phase 1, so the example claim's `inventory_sha256` is final when it is written.
Output: policy and claim documents with their checks and six inventory rows. PROJECT.md decisions, README links and the final CI evidence follow in plan 01-13.
</objective>
