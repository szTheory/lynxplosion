## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** developer, **I want to** have the 65C02-class CPU execute under data-defined Lynx I and Lynx II profiles, proven by pinned per-opcode vectors, **so that** later device phases build on a CPU whose behaviour is checked per profile without either profile being adopted as baseline.

<objective>
Create the two programs the machine switch re-baselines on (CONTEXT "Re-baselining Phase 1 on purpose": a never-ending loop of shared opcodes reading seeded RAM, and a short terminating program with a committed, hand-derived and cited trace table; the pinned `ca65` re-assembles the committed `.s` and must match the committed hex; CONTEXT amendment: one `check.sh` step plus a committed linker `.cfg`, no generator scripts).

Purpose: independent expectations for plan 02-09 (registers, RAM and ticks derived by hand, not by the CPU).
Output: five fixture files, two manifests, the `cpu-fixtures` check step and two inventory rows.
</objective>
