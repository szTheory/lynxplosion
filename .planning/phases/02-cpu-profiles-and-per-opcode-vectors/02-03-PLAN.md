## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** developer, **I want to** have the 65C02-class CPU execute under data-defined Lynx I and Lynx II profiles, proven by pinned per-opcode vectors, **so that** later device phases build on a CPU whose behaviour is checked per profile without either profile being adopted as baseline.

<objective>
Commit the pinned vector sample the fast gate runs (CONTEXT "Vector sourcing, inventory and controls": sources, pin, committed sample of the first 32 vectors per opcode file, no `pins.txt` row, third-party MIT data recorded in a manifest, provenance and notices), produced reproducibly by `scripts/vectors-full.sh`.

Purpose: CPU-01 needs pinned per-opcode vectors in the check without network access.
Output: the full-lane script (pin and sample modes), two sample containers, the upstream LICENSE, the 512-row pin record, `.gitattributes`, two manifests and the provenance section.
</objective>
