## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** developer, **I want to** have the 65C02-class CPU execute under data-defined Lynx I and Lynx II profiles, proven by pinned per-opcode vectors, **so that** later device phases build on a CPU whose behaviour is checked per profile without either profile being adopted as baseline.

<objective>
Cover what SingleStepTests cannot (ROADMAP criterion 4: IRQ entry, CLI/SEI latency, WAI/STP and reserved-opcode NOP lengths, each with a cited source or marked unverified), with the CONTEXT "Own scenario vectors" list and the directed edges of RESEARCH 5.7.

Purpose: CPU-04 evidence plus the edges the sample cannot reach (RESEARCH R9, OQ10).
Output: `tests/cpu/test_cpu_scenarios.c` and four inventory rows. CPU defects found here are fixed in `core/cpu.c`.
</objective>
